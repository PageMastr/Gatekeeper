# ROADMAP — {{NAME}}

> The ordered list of phases from here to a game you can hand someone. Written
> by `/gd:plan` at kickoff, revised at every `/gd:ship`.
>
> Rules this list obeys:
> - **Phase 1 is always the greybox.** The loop gets proven before anything is
>   made to look good, because art on a missing loop is wasted art.
> - **Each phase has a machine-readable gate** — the `- gate:` lines in its
>   `PLAN.md`. If you cannot write the gate, the phase is not defined yet.
> - **Each phase ends with something playable**, not with a finished layer. A
>   phase that ends in "the kit is done, nothing uses it" cannot be judged.
> - **Order by risk, not by comfort.** The thing most likely to kill the project
>   goes early, while changing your mind is still cheap.
